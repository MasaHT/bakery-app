Jumlop Bakery phone app — short guide
=====================================

The official app is:

  https://masaht.github.io/bakery-app/

Open that exact link in Chrome. Do not add ?mock=1 at the end.
If you see an orange "Practice mode" badge at the top, you are on the
wrong link. Close that page and open the official link above.

1. Put it on your Home screen (like a normal phone app)
-------------------------------------------------------
On Android, open the official link in Chrome.
- If you see a bar that says "Add Jumlop to your Home screen so it
  opens like a phone app", tap "Add to Home screen".
- If you do not see that bar, tap Chrome's menu (three dots) and tap
  "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
- You can tap "Not now" if you want to do this later.

After that, open Jumlop from the icon on your Home screen.

2. Name and Family PIN (only once on each phone)
------------------------------------------------
The app first asks "Who's entering?" Tap your name (Mummy, Papa,
Pascal, Lovemore, Masa, Godfrey), or type another name and tap
"Use name".

It then asks for the "Family PIN". This is a 6-digit number. Ask Masa
for it. Type it and tap "Save PIN".

The phone remembers your name and the PIN, so you should not have to
enter them again.

3. Enter the day's numbers
--------------------------
At the top you will see "Entering as:" and your name.

Pick a tab:
- Wholesale Entry  (deliveries, stock left, cash, handovers)
- Expenses
- Production
- Dispatch room

Check the date at the top ("Date (Malawi)"). You can tap Today,
Yesterday, or 2 days ago.

On Wholesale Entry, also check the Shop name (Town, Home, Wakawaka,
or St. John's). The app remembers the last shop used on that phone,
so always look before you type.

Type the numbers, then tap the dark button at the bottom:
- "Save" on Wholesale Entry
- "Save all expenses" on Expenses
- "Save production" on Production
- "Save count" on Dispatch room

If it worked, a green message appears, for example
"Saved 25/11/2026 (Home)". The phone may also give a short buzz.

4. Switching names on a shared phone
------------------------------------
If someone else needs to enter on the same phone, tap "Change" next
to "Entering as:". Pick the new name. That person's entries will be
saved under the new name.

5. The "Enter PIN" button
-------------------------
"Enter PIN" does not change the family's real PIN. It only lets you
type the PIN again on this phone, for example if Masa has given you
a new Family PIN, or if the last try was typed wrong.

If a PIN is already saved, you can tap "Keep current PIN" to cancel.

6. If something goes wrong
--------------------------
If it says "That PIN is not right. Ask Masa for the Family PIN and
try again.": ask Masa, then type the 6-digit PIN again and tap
"Save PIN".

If you see "No internet — numbers can't load or save right now":
the app can still be opened, but it cannot load or save numbers
until the phone is back online. Check the signal, then try again
and look for the green saved message.

If the page looks old after an update: close the app completely,
open it again from the Home screen icon, and pull down only after
you have finished typing (so you do not lose numbers you have not
saved yet).
